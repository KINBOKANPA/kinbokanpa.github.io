Questions

Before you book

Do I need to know anyone else on the trip?

Not at all — most travelers join solo or in pairs. Groups are kept small so it never feels anonymous.

What's not included in the price?

International flights to the starting city, travel insurance, and personal spending are on you — everything else is covered.

Can I request a custom trip for my friend group?

Yes — the Custom Group option lets you pick the route and dates for a private departure of 6–12 people.
